Health Guide Cards

Heart Related Guide

Keeping your heart healthy requires a combination of daily movement, nutritious eating, and routine medical monitoring. The most impactful steps include quitting tobacco, managing stress, getting 7 to 9 hours of sleep nightly, and maintaining a healthy weight.


Brain Related Guide

Keeping your brain healthy relies on a combination of active learning, regular physical movement, heart-healthy nutrition, and restorative sleep. Together, these habits promote the growth of new neural connections.
